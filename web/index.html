Three quick tips

  1. Film each room or thing you'll talk about for 5 to 10 seconds, holding the phone steady.
  2. Record your voice-over as a voice memo, under a minute. Say a little about each thing you filmed.
  3. Nothing posts by itself. You watch your video, change anything you like, then post it yourself.
